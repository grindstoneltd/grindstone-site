A grindstone is the tool that keeps every other tool sharp. That's the job we've set ourselves: to give contract manufacturers and their customers sharper ways to find each other, run the work and fix the problems that slow it down. The name is also a reminder of how we work: practical, hands-on, and nose to the grindstone until the job is done.

## What Grindstone does

- **EMS Directory.** [EMS Directory](https://www.emsdirectory.co.uk/) lists 370+ UK contract electronics manufacturers in one format, so buyers can compare them on capability and certification and send one RFQ to their whole shortlist.
- **drumbeat+.** Sales and operations software for EMS companies. It takes every job from enquiry to delivery and runs beside the ERP you already have.
- **Grindstone Consultancy.** Hands-on help with winning the right work, new product introduction, customer approvals, quality problems and practical AI, remotely or on your shop floor. [See what we can help with]({gemba_page}).

## Who is behind it

Grindstone was founded by Colin Astbury, who has spent over thirty years in engineering and manufacturing, from the Royal Navy's Fleet Air Arm to 18 years at TT Electronics and operations roles across OEMs and Tier 1 suppliers. [Connect with Colin on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/colinastbury/).
