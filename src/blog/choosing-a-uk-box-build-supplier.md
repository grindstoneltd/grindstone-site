A supplier can be excellent at board assembly and still be a different proposition for box build. Enclosures, cabling, mechanical fixing, protection and final product test need different skills, suppliers and equipment.

This EMS Directory guide sets out what to ask before you commit a box build to anyone, using figures from the 243 UK box build suppliers it lists. It covers agreeing the scope in writing, checking how mechanical assembly and fastening are controlled, and which environmental protection steps run in-house. It also covers what final product testing should prove, the sourcing and shipping model, matching the supplier to your volumes, and asking for examples in your sector. It ends with a quick-reference checklist.

**Why it matters:** box build quotes are hard to compare, because "box build" means something different to every supplier. A written scope, agreed test coverage and clear ownership of long-lead mechanical parts avoid most of the surprises that turn up at first article.
