A certificate tells you a supplier has a quality system. It doesn't tell you whether their processes, testing and supply chain fit your product. This guide from EMS Directory works through how to close that gap.

It starts with the baseline certifications every buyer should expect, then the sector-specific ones that matter for aerospace, medical, automotive and defence work. From there it moves to the detail a certificate can't show: how deep the testing and inspection goes, how component sourcing and supply-chain risk are managed, and which facility and process questions to ask in person.

It finishes with a quick-reference checklist you can take into a supplier visit.

**Why it matters:** choosing the wrong partner is one of the most expensive mistakes in an electronics programme, and it usually shows up late, at first article or during ramp-up. Supplier selection and qualification is one of the things [Grindstone Consultancy]({gemba_page}) helps with.
