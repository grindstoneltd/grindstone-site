Grindstone Ltd launched [EMC Directory](https://www.emcdirectory.co.uk/) on 29 October 2026. It lists 30+ UK EMC test labs and consultancies, from pre-compliance scans to accredited compliance testing. It's built the same way as [EMS Directory](https://www.emsdirectory.co.uk/) and [CNC Directory](https://www.cncdirectory.co.uk/), our directories of UK contract electronics manufacturers and CNC machining companies.

Two labs can both say "EMC testing" and do very different work. One runs pre-compliance scans on consumer products in a screened room; another holds UKAS accreditation and tests to MIL-STD-461 and DEF STAN 59-411 in a fully anechoic chamber. EMC Directory shows the details that decide the fit in the same layout on every listing:

- **Standards covered**, from EN 55032 and the EN 61000-4 series to MIL-STD-461, DEF STAN 59-411 and RTCA DO-160
- **Accreditations** such as UKAS accreditation to ISO/IEC 17025
- **Test types and facilities**, from radiated and conducted emissions and immunity to ESD, with chamber types and frequency range
- **Industries supported**, and whether the lab can test on your site

You can search by standard, accreditation and location. Every listing has a Last Updated date and a Send Email button, so you can reach the lab directly.

**Why it matters:** booking the right lab early means fewer surprises at compliance and a faster route to market. Getting new products through approval is part of the work [Grindstone]({gemba_page}) does.
