Grindstone Ltd launched [Contract Manufacturing UK](https://www.contractmanufacturing.co.uk/) on 9 November 2026. It brings together the companies on our specialist directories, including [EMS Directory](https://www.emsdirectory.co.uk/), [CNC Directory](https://www.cncdirectory.co.uk/), [EMC Directory](https://www.emcdirectory.co.uk/) and [PCB Directory](https://www.pcbdirectory.co.uk/), so buyers and engineers can search more than 1,000 UK companies in one place.

At its heart is Chip, a free assistant. Tell Chip what you need made or tested. It asks a few simple questions, shortlists suitable suppliers, then builds and sends your request for quotation for you, or emails you the links.

Finding the right partner usually means searching process by process and region by region, then writing the same enquiry several times. Contract Manufacturing UK puts that search in one place, across electronics manufacturing, CNC machining, PCB, 3D printing and test services, and lets buyers browse by process, location and sector.

"Buyers shouldn't need to spend hours searching online to find their UK contract manufacturing partner. Contract Manufacturing UK lets them describe the job in their own words and get a shortlist of UK companies that do that work, quickly and free of charge," said Colin Astbury, Director of Grindstone Ltd.

The service is free for buyers. Companies listed on any of our directories are included automatically.
